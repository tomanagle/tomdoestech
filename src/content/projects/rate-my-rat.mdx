## Context & Background
HoundHouse made canvas dog houses and had a batch of small versions that were marketed as "rat houses". To my surprise, there were communities of rat owners who were very proud of their pets. I wanted to get the community involved and introduce the rat houses to them.

## Implementation
I built a Drupal site where rat lovers could register and upload a picture of their rats. Other users could swipe through the community-generated images and rate them. I also added a leaderboard, then built a single-page site for the rat houses and advertised it in rat communities.

## What Went Well
The rat-house landing page recorded its first purchase within 20 minutes of the first advertising post.
