## Context & Background
M&D was running a Drupal 5 e-commerce website that was slow, difficult to maintain and not mobile-friendly. The goal was to upgrade the website to the latest version of Drupal, which at the time was 7.

## Implementation
We chose a theme and built the site on a local desktop that every computer in the office could access. We customised the theme where necessary with PHP and CSS, then built a custom module to import products from XML files.

## What Went Well
The website was launched and ran successfully on an AWS EC2 instance. It was mobile-friendly and much faster than the previous version.

At the time there weren't many good options for e-commerce sites that needed custom functionality. Shopify was still fairly immature and WooCommerce didn't provide an easy path for customisation. The team was already familiar with Drupal, so I think sticking with it despite its flaws was the right choice.

## Outcome
- Mobile-friendly e-commerce website.
- Improved performance and SEO.
