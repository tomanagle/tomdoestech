## Context & Background

When customers purchase a solar system, battery or EV charger, they need details about what they are buying, which rebates and loans apply, and how the installation is progressing. The existing portal was split across two applications, one Vue 2 and one Nuxt, and did not explain that information clearly.

I worked with a subject matter expert (SME) and a designer to put the checkout and installation journey in one portal.

## Implementation

We kept the existing APIs and extended them where required without breaking the old portal. I built the new portal using Vite, React, Tanstack Router and Tanstack Query.

Because the portal is a key part of the sales process, I added a feature flag that let operators choose what share of new customers received each version. We could start with a randomly selected group, and each customer stayed on the same version for the rest of their journey.

I also built operator tooling to show which version a customer was using and switch them when required.

## What Went Well

The new portal put the contract requirements and installation process in one place, so customers could see what they needed to provide and what would happen next.

Its checkout components are modular, so new government loans and rebates can be added without rebuilding the rest of checkout.
